SPACE SUMO

A cosmic knockout

Guide

Your goal is brutally simple: stay on the planet and knock everyone else off. Use movement, boost, and power-ups to outplay other players. If you drift too far from the planet or get launched into space, you're done.

Controls

W / A / S / D — Move around the planet’s surface (tangential movement).
Space — Boost upward away from the planet for a quick reposition or to avoid being knocked off.
E — Push attack (short-range impulse that shoves nearby players). Use it aggressively or to finish someone who’s off-balance.

Win Condition

Last player still on or near the planet wins. Anyone who crosses the out-of-bounds distance is instantly eliminated—no second chances.

Power-Ups

Power-ups spawn periodically on the planet surface. Rolling over them grants temporary advantages:

• Turbo Core — Massive boost strength for a short duration. Great for escaping or launching someone into orbit.
• Heavy Shell — Increases your mass, making you harder to push while making your pushes hit harder.
• Slipstream — Lowers friction for faster lateral movement, but makes controlling momentum riskier.
• Shock Pulse — Your next push (E) releases an enhanced radial blast with increased knockback.
